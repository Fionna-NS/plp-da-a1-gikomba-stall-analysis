## Business Problem

Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market. For the last two weeks she has recorded her daily sales in a notebook and kept a rough record of her stock. She wants to know how her business is doing, whether it is growing, which products she should restock, and where she should focus her effort. This project uses core Python only (lists, dictionaries, functions and f-strings, with no pandas or NumPy) to analyse 14 days of sales and her stock levels and turn them into clear recommendations.

Then choose **Runtime → Run all** to reproduce the Stall Performance Report.

## Top Three Findings

1. **Weekends earn far more:** weekend days average KES 4,900 versus KES 2,685 on weekdays, which is 82.50% higher.
2. **The business is growing:** sales rose from KES 22,050 in week 1 to KES 24,400 in week 2, an increase of 10.66%.
3. **Stock is running low on two products:** Tomatoes (18 kg vs a reorder level of 25 kg) and Sukuma (9 kg vs 15 kg) are below their reorder levels, so both need restocking.
